Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications published by Michael Clanton that reference this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The applications covered by this Privacy Policy do not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information from users.

The applications do not require users to provide personal information such as an email address, phone number, precise location, or payment information directly to the developer.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Some applications may store limited game or application information locally on your device, such as:

  • Player or team names
  • Game scores
  • Local leaderboard information
  • Game progress
  • Preferences or settings

This information remains locally on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or a developer-operated external server.

Deleting the application or clearing its locally stored data may remove this information.

3. Analytics

The current versions of the applications covered by this policy do not use third-party analytics services to monitor user behavior.

4. Advertising

The current versions of the applications covered by this policy do not contain third-party advertising or advertising SDKs.

If advertising is introduced in a future version of an application, this Privacy Policy and the applicable app-store privacy disclosures will be updated as appropriate.

5. Tracking

The applications covered by this policy do not track users across applications, websites, or services owned by other companies.

They do not currently use advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking.

6. Location Information

The applications covered by this policy do not request, collect, or use precise or approximate geographic location unless specifically disclosed within an individual application.

7. Camera, Microphone, Photos, and Contacts

The current applications covered by this policy do not require access to the camera, microphone, photos, or contacts as part of their normal operation unless specifically disclosed within an individual application.

8. User Accounts

The current applications covered by this policy do not require users to create developer-operated user accounts unless specifically stated within an individual application.

9. Purchases

If an application offers purchases or subscriptions through an app store, payment transactions are handled by the applicable app-store provider.

The developer does not directly receive a user's complete payment-card information from the app-store provider.

10. Children's Privacy

The applications covered by this policy do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the current applications do not transmit personal information to developer-operated servers, the developer does not intentionally maintain personal information about children.

11. Third-Party Services

The current versions of the applications covered by this policy do not intentionally transmit personal information to third-party advertising or analytics providers.

Platform providers such as Apple may independently process information associated with downloading, installing, updating, purchasing, or using applications in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies.

12. Data Sharing and Sale

The developer does not sell or rent personal information collected through the applications covered by this Privacy Policy.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the current applications do not maintain personal information on developer-controlled servers, there is generally no server-stored personal information for the developer to delete.

Locally stored application data can generally be removed by deleting the application or clearing application data where supported by the operating system.

14. Security

The applications are designed to minimize the collection and transmission of personal information.

No method of electronic storage or software operation can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
